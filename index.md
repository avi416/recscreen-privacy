# Privacy Policy for RecScreen

**Last updated: 12 October 2026**

## The short version

RecScreen does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no account system,
no analytics, no advertising, and no network permission. Everything the app produces stays on
your device.

## What RecScreen accesses, and why

**Screen contents.** When you start a recording, Android asks for your explicit permission
to capture the screen, and shows a persistent indicator for as long as capture is running.
RecScreen writes what it captures to a video file on your device. It is never transmitted.

Please remember that a recording contains whatever was on your screen while it ran — which
can include passwords as you type them, banking details, messages and notifications. Review
a recording before you share it.

**Audio played by your device.** RecScreen records the audio your device is playing, so that
recordings have sound. Android requires the `RECORD_AUDIO` permission to do this, even though
**RecScreen never records from your microphone**. The permission is used solely for playback
capture. If you set Audio to None in Settings, no audio is captured at all.

**Storage.** Recordings are saved to your device's shared video storage, under `Movies`, so
they appear in your gallery. RecScreen can see the recordings it created and any files in its
own output folder matching its own naming; it does not browse your wider media library.

**Notifications.** Used to show the recording controls while a recording is running.

**Display over other apps.** Used only to draw the optional floating control button and the
countdown, over whatever is on screen.

## What RecScreen does not do

- It has no `INTERNET` permission. It is technically incapable of sending anything anywhere.
- It does not collect analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or usage statistics.
- It does not have accounts, sign-in, or cloud sync.
- It does not read your recordings for any purpose beyond showing them in its own library.
- It does not attempt to bypass apps that forbid their audio being captured. When an app sets
  such a policy, RecScreen tells you rather than working around it.

## Diagnostics

RecScreen keeps a local diagnostic log of recording attempts, to explain failures. It records
what the app did — which stage a recording reached, the device model, Android version,
permission states and capture settings. **It stays on your device**, is only shown when you
open Settings → Diagnostics, and is only ever shared if you choose to copy or share it
yourself. It contains no recording content and no personal data.

## Your recordings

Your recordings belong to you. They are stored in your device's normal video storage and can
be moved, shared or deleted like any other video. Uninstalling RecScreen does not delete
recordings already saved to your gallery.

## Children

RecScreen is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
